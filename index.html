THE LABORATORY / 001

Curiosity is the instrument.

Anomaly Works is an independent experimental laboratory exploring intelligence, observation, and unexpected discovery. We begin with questions, build small instruments, and let real-world tests challenge the explanations.

01 / THE QUESTION

Look differently.

What happens when human and artificial intelligence investigate something ordinary without assuming where the answer should lead?

02 / THE WORKBENCH

Build. Test. Reconsider.

Small experimental instruments, practical objects, observation protocols, and prototypes. An idea can become a working tool, change shape entirely, or be set aside.

03 / FIELD NOTES
