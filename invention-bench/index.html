01 / FIRST ON THE BENCH · CONCEPT

Slow Fuse

A gravity-powered timer that triggers a latch after a delay. No battery, screen, or electronics. The timing regulator is the central engineering challenge.

First question: Can a small weight and a simple passive regulator deliver a useful, repeatable delay without excessive complexity?

Slow Fuse

Grip Back

One-way rope or webbing connector that slides and locks.

Test: varied textiles, grip, release and fiber wear.
03 / 11CONCEPT

Thaw Witness

Passive record of a meaningful freezer thaw/refreeze event.

Test: threshold and duration; false positives.
04 / 19CONCEPT

Shock Count

Mechanical counter for repeated above-threshold impacts.

Test: separate shocks from everyday vibration.
05 / 07CONCEPT

Pressure Memory

A passive indicator of the greatest applied force.

Test: accuracy, reset, and mechanical hysteresis.
06 / 08CONCEPT

Emergency Connector

Low-part-count temporary repair fastening system.

Test: compatibility with common materials.
07 / 03CONCEPT

Heat Witness

Visible, persistent indication of an over-temperature event.

Test: trigger point and repeatability.
08 / 16CONCEPT

Draft Finder

Small handheld indicator for detecting unwanted airflow.

Test: sensitivity and drafts vs room turbulence.
09 / 15CONCEPT

Tank Truth

External indication of fluid level in opaque containers.

10 / 05CONCEPT
