# Anomaly Works — Invention Lab 002

**Added:** 2026-11-02. Initial ideas only; novelty and feasibility are unverified.

| # | Name | Concept | Key engineering challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Thaw Witness | Reusable freezer indicator that records a thaw/refreeze event | Reliable temperature threshold, duration, reset |
| 12 | Sun Dose | Passive indicator for sunlight exposure during solar water disinfection | Exposure alone cannot establish water safety; intensity, cloud cover, clarity, microbial validation |
| 13 | Slow Fuse | Gravity-driven delayed mechanical release | Timing consistency, orientation, friction |
| 14 | Flood Stop | Float-actuated water shutoff for leaks without electrical power | Reliable upstream shutoff, pressure range, false trips, code compliance |
| 15 | Tank Truth | External level gauge for opaque tanks | Material dependence, tank geometry, calibration |
| 16 | Draft Finder | Lightweight local airflow leak locator | Sensitivity, false airflow, ease of use |
| 17 | Solo Lift | Compact lift-and-hold lever | Structural safety, locking stability, pinch/crush risk |
| 18 | Grip Back | One-way rope or webbing grip | Fiber damage, varied sizes/materials, release under load |
| 19 | Shock Count | Mechanical count of threshold-exceeding impacts | Distinguishing true impacts from vibrations and orientation artifacts |
| 20 | The Small Bet | Physical decision-testing aid | Encourage falsifiable low-cost trials instead of vague motivational prompts |

## First review

Strongest exploration candidates: **11 Thaw Witness**, **18 Grip Back**, and **19 Shock Count**. Existing products and mechanisms must be reviewed before any novelty claims. #12 must not imply potable-water safety from a mere sunlight reading. #17 and #14 entail significant mechanical safety and reliability requirements.
