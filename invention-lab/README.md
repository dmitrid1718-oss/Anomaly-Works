# Anomaly Works — Invention Lab 001

**Started:** 2026-11-02  
**Cadence:** One new invention experiment per week  
**Approach:** Paper-clip-simple, useful, cheap to prototype, documented openly.

These are **initial concepts**, not verified inventions or claims of novelty. Before any commercialization, examine prior art, safety, usability, cost, and actual user demand.

| # | Working title | Problem | First prototype / mechanism to investigate | First test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Gravity Flag | Tell if a door, cabinet or object has moved | Pivoting weighted flag with a one-way latch, reset by hand | Distinguish real movement from vibration |
| 02 | Last Drop | Dispense small amounts of water without power | Gravity-fed reservoir with simple mechanical metering / shutoff | Measure repeatability and leaks |
| 03 | Heat Witness | Reveal if equipment exceeded a temperature threshold | Bimetal-actuated latch and persistent visual flag | Calibrate trigger and reset across cycles |
| 04 | One-Hand Clamp | Hold materials while both hands are occupied | Self-wedging cam with spring return | Grip range, speed, force, surface damage |
| 05 | Silent Leak | Detect water leakage without batteries | Swelling element or float that releases a mechanical signal flag/bell | Detection time, false triggers, resetability |
| 06 | Level Anywhere | Make adjustable supports from scrap material | Folding wedge and detent arrangement | Stability under load, adjustment steps |
| 07 | Pressure Memory | Preserve evidence of peak force without electronics | Sliding indicator with friction or ratchet driven by spring/lever | Compare peak-force indication to known loads |
| 08 | Emergency Connector | Make fast repairs with common materials | Universal folded strap / cam / friction-lock connector | Trial on wood, sheet, fabric, cord; safe load limits |
| 09 | Thermal Switch | Trigger venting/release based on ambient temperature without power | Bimetal strip or wax actuator with mechanical latch | Trigger precision, hysteresis, durability |
| 10 | The Unlocked Door | Help someone test a long-held assumption | Pocket-sized physical hypothesis/test card device with reveal mechanism | Does it prompt a safe, falsifiable experiment? |

## Weekly invention record

Each week's individual page should record: **problem**, **existing solutions / prior art**, **mechanism**, **hand sketch / CAD**, **bill of materials**, **prototype**, **observed test results**, **failure modes**, **revision**, and **public comments**. Report failures, not just successes.

## Ground rules

- No gimmick patents or novelty claims before checking prior art.
- Favor simple physical mechanisms and common materials; aim for sub-$50 first prototypes where practical.
- Do not imply a device is safe for potable water, electrical installations, load-bearing use, medical use, or emergency reliance without appropriate testing and certification.
- One experiment per week, even if the outcome is **not viable**.
- Link this archive from the public Anomaly Works site when website navigation is updated.
